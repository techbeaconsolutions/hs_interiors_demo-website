Our Process

How We Work

A streamlined 5-step process designed to deliver your dream space on time and within budget.

1. Consultation

We begin with understanding your vision, requirements, budget, and timeline. Our designers visit your space for accurate measurements.

2. Design Planning

Our team creates detailed floor plans, mood boards, and material selections tailored to your style and functional needs.

3. 3D Visualization

Experience your space before construction with photorealistic 3D renders. Make changes and finalize every detail.

4. Execution

Skilled craftsmen bring designs to life with precision. Regular site visits and quality checks ensure perfection.

5. Final Delivery

Handover of your beautifully designed space with warranty documentation and maintenance guidelines.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have got answers. Here are some common queries from our clients.

Interior design costs in Pune typically range from Rs. 800 to Rs. 2,500 per square foot depending on the scope, materials, and complexity. A basic 2BHK (800 sq ft) can start from Rs. 6-8 lakhs, while premium villas can go up to Rs. 50+ lakhs. We provide detailed quotations after understanding your requirements during the free consultation.

Project timelines vary based on size and complexity. A typical 2BHK interior takes 45-60 days, a 3BHK takes 60-90 days, and villas or commercial spaces can take 3-6 months. We provide a detailed timeline during the planning phase and ensure strict adherence to deadlines.

Yes! We provide photorealistic 3D renders for all our projects. You can visualize your space before any construction begins, make changes to colors, materials, and layouts, and approve every detail. This ensures there are no surprises and you get exactly what you envision.

Absolutely! We specialize in turnkey interior solutions where we handle everything from design to execution. This includes civil work, electrical, plumbing, carpentry, painting, and final furnishing. You just hand over the keys and we deliver a ready-to-move-in space.

We primarily serve Pune city and nearby areas including PCMC, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Hinjewadi, Kharadi, Baner, Aundh, Wakad, Magarpatta, Kalyani Nagar, Koregaon Park, Viman Nagar, Kothrud, and surrounding regions. We also take select projects in Mumbai and nearby cities.

Yes, we offer a comprehensive warranty on all our work. Carpentry and modular furniture comes with a 5-year warranty, electrical work with 2 years, and painting with 1 year. We also provide 6 months of free maintenance service after project handover.
